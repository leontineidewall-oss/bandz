    06 · faq

    questions.

    what is bandz?

    a coin with a city. every buy pulls a job, every sell brings cops, and the coin's fees stack in a safehouse that pays the crew.

    when is payday?

    when the heat is 0★: the last hour had clearly more buys than sells, or it was quiet. at most once every few hours.

    who gets paid?

    every wallet holding at least the floor of — bandz, split by bag size. pools, bonding curves and the safehouse itself never count.

    how is it paid?

    in sol, straight from the safehouse wallet to yours. every payday is a public transaction listed under safehouse.

    what is lockdown?

    5★. so many sells that the city is locked down. no payday until the heat cools.

    where do the numbers come from?

    dexscreener for price and heat, geckoterminal for jobs, solana itself for the safehouse and the crew. a dash means no data yet. it never means zero.
